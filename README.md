## UAE Permit & Approval Process

### Step 1: Developer NOC
Required in all master-planned communities (Emaar, Nakheel, Meraas, DAMAC, Aldar).
- Submit: pool drawings, contractor license, insurance
- Timeline: 3–6 weeks
- Cost: AED 500–2,000 (varies by developer)

### Step 2: Municipality Permit
- **Dubai:** Dubai Municipality structural permit
- **Abu Dhabi:** Abu Dhabi Municipality engineering permit
- **RAK:** RAK Municipality approval
- Timeline: 2–4 weeks after developer NOC

### Step 3: DEWA Approval
Required for electrical connections (pump, chiller, lighting).
- Submit: electrical drawings, load calculations
- Timeline: 1–2 weeks

### Step 4: Construction
- Excavation and structural works
- Waterproofing (critical — use certified waterproofing systems)
- Plumbing and filtration installation
- Tiling and finishing
- Equipment installation and commissioning

### Step 5: Completion
- Municipality inspection
- DEWA connection
- Pool commissioning and water treatment

## UAE Climate Considerations

### Summer (May–October)
- Unshaded pools reach 35–40°C — unswimmable without cooling
- **Chiller is mandatory** for year-round use
- Reversible heat pump handles both cooling (summer) and heating (winter)
- Evaporation rate: 5–8mm/day — budget for water top-up

### Winter (November–April)
- Water temperature drops to 18–22°C — heating required for comfort
- Reversible heat pump handles heating efficiently
- Ideal construction season (cooler temperatures, faster curing)

### Year-Round
- High dust — pool covers recommended
- Hard water — salt chlorination systems reduce chemical costs
- UV intensity — use UV-resistant pool finishes and equipment

## Choosing a Pool Contractor in UAE

### Must-Have Criteria
- [ ] Dubai Municipality or relevant authority registration
- [ ] Proven pool construction portfolio (request photos and references)
- [ ] In-house structural, waterproofing, and MEP capability
- [ ] Experience with developer NOC process in your community
- [ ] Warranty on waterproofing (minimum 5 years)
- [ ] Warranty on equipment (minimum 2 years)

### Questions to Ask
1. Who manages the permit applications?
2. What waterproofing system do you use and what is the warranty?
3. Do you include a chiller/heat pump in the quote?
4. What is the filtration system specification?
5. Do you provide a maintenance contract after completion?
